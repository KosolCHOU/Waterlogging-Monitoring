🌾 About CropXcel

⚠️ Pain Points We Solve

For many Cambodian farmers, waterlogging is a silent thief. Heavy rains flood the fields, the rice turns yellow, and weeks of hard work can disappear in days.

  • Unseen water stress: Hidden water stays in soil; damage shows up late.
  • Late decisions: By the time yellow patches appear, yield is already lost.
  • Lack of guidance: Farmers see problems but don’t know the next step.

CropXcel gives farmers eyes in the sky 🌍 — early warnings before it’s too late.

✨ How CropXcel Helps You

We use Sentinel-1 & Sentinel-2 to monitor your fields every few days and turn complex signals into clear, simple insights:

  • Know which areas are Healthy, need Attention, or require Action.
  • Receive Water Advice Cards 💧 — drain, reduce irrigation, or keep routine.
  • Track changes over time to protect your harvest early.

📊 Analytics at a Glance New

The Analytics page turns weather and satellite signals into farmer-friendly guidance:

  • Weather today & next 72h rain signal — plan spraying, fertilizing, and drainage.
  • 7-day forecast table — quickly spot heavy rain, wind, heat, or dry spells.
  • Recommendations — simple actions you can take now to protect yield.

Tip: Open any field and tap Analytics to see risks and advice for that field.

🗺️ How to Use the Dashboard

  • Map View: Colors show water risk — green safe, yellow warning, red danger.
  • Per-Pass Insights: Each satellite pass is like a diary entry for your field.
  • Analysis Scale (Donut): See how much of your field is healthy vs risky.
  • History Plot: Watch risk trends for smarter planning.

🌱 Crop Recommendation

Choosing the right crop for your soil and season is just as important as managing water. Our Crop Compass analyzes your soil nutrients (N, P, K, pH) and weather conditions (temperature, humidity, rainfall) to suggest the top 3 best matches for your land. This helps you reduce risks, improve yield, and plan with confidence.

  • Smart suggestions: see which varieties fit your soil & climate best.
  • Data-driven: no more guessing — use science to guide your choices.
  • Farmer-friendly: results shown simply, with clear confidence scores.

🚀 Our Vision

CropXcel is more than a tool — it’s a step toward smart farming in Cambodia. We aim to empower farmers with technology that speaks their language, helping them grow healthier crops, earn better incomes, and protect their land for the future. Together, we can turn data into better harvests 🌾.
